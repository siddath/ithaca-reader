# Departure

Read after [Arrival](arrival.md). This example uses Arabic chapter numbers and custom section names.

## Story {#story}

[T] A visitor returns the lantern and explains that it guided him to the steps. Mara and her brother clean it. The brother takes it out that evening.

## Quotations {#quotations}

> That evening, he carried the lantern.

[I] The final action answers his earlier question through participation. The narrator never gives us his thoughts.

## Craft {#craft}

[I] Cleaning the glass is a small act shared by both characters. It makes the object reusable and quietly transfers responsibility.

## Questions {#questions}

- Why does the story omit the stranger’s journey?
- Does the brother need proof before he can act, or did his thinking begin the evening before?
