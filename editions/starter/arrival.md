# Arrival

An original example. The source text is separate from this commentary.

## Story {#story}

[T] Mara lights an empty harbour after the last scheduled boat has gone. Her brother initially sees no use for it, but stays with her.

## Quotations {#quotations}

> Someone coming later than they meant to,

[I] “Someone” leaves the imagined visitor unnamed. The light can be useful without its keeper knowing who will arrive.

## Reading notes {#reading-notes}

[I] The lantern can be read as hospitality offered before a guest appears. This is an interpretation supported by Mara’s explanation, rather than a claim that every light in fiction has this meaning.

[A] Her brother’s question also admits a practical reading: he is asking whether a task has a purpose. The story leaves his motive unstated.

## Questions {#questions}

- Does staying beside Mara amount to agreement?
- What changes if the light has no witness?
